About this Space

Welcome to Millennium Binghatti Residences – Studio Apartment, developed by Binghatti Developers, where modern design meets convenience in the heart of Business Bay.

Enjoy a thoughtfully designed studio with an open layout, stylish interiors, and a balcony that frames the iconic Burj Khalifa alongside the Dubai Canal. Perfect for a weekend getaway or a short business trip.

The Space

  • Size: 49.60 sq.m (534 sq.ft)
  • Open-plan living with sofa & Smart TV
  • Sleeping area with premium bed & linens
  • Fully equipped kitchenette (fridge, oven, microwave, cooker, kettle, cookware & cutlery)
  • 1 Modern bathroom with towels & essentials
  • Balcony with Burj Khalifa & Canal views

Why Stay Here?

Located in Business Bay, this studio places you just 5 minutes by car (≈ 2–3 km) from Downtown Dubai, the Dubai Mall, and the DIFC, while still offering a quieter canal-side setting. Ideal for couples, solo travelers, or professionals seeking convenience.
