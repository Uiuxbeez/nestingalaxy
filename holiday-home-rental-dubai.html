FAQs – Holiday Home Rental Dubai

1. Are Nest in Galaxy’s homes licensed for holiday stays?

Yes, every property is registered and licensed under Dubai’s Tourism regulations for short-term stays.

2. What’s included in the holiday home booking?

All utilities like Wi-Fi, DEWA, cleaning services, and full furnishings are included. You just have to bring your suitcase.

3. Can I expect a long-stay option?

Yes, you can. Though our focus is on short-term rentals, many of our premium properties are best suited for extended stays.

4. Are these properties suitable for working from home?

Yes. Most of our holiday homes are well equipped for remote work. You get high-speed Wi-Fi, comfortable workspaces, and a peaceful environment. We support your productivity without compromising on luxury.

5. Can I book on behalf of a company?

Absolutely. We support corporate bookings and offer customized invoicing and contracts.

6. Are your rentals legally approved?

Yes, every Nest in Galaxy property is fully licensed and compliant with Dubai Tourism’s short-term rental guidelines.
