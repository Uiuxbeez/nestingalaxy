FAQs – Executive Short-Term Flat Rentals

1. Are utilities included in the monthly rent?

Yes, all bills (DEWA, internet, cooling) are included. No hidden costs.

2. Can I bring a driver or nanny along?

Many flats in our portfolio include maid’s rooms or studio annexes. Let us know your needs.

3. Do you offer parking for rented flats?

Yes, most of our properties include private or secured basement parking.

4. Can I get a flat near a specific school, hospital, or office?

Of course. Our team will shortlist options within proximity to your preferred landmarks.

5. Can I extend my stay if my plans change?

Usually, yes. Early communication helps us guarantee availability.

6. Can I host small gatherings or client meetings in the flat?

Many properties allow this with prior notice, especially for longer stays.

7. Can I book flats for my relocating employees?

Absolutely. We work with corporates and HR teams to provide turnkey housing with flexible terms and invoicing.
