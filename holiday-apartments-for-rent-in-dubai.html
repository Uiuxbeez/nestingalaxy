FAQs – Planning Your Dubai Holiday Stay

1. Is cleaning included in the rental?

Most holiday homes offer weekly or bi-weekly cleaning. Daily cleaning can be arranged on request.

2. Can I bring pets to the holiday apartment?

Some listings are pet-friendly. Use the filter to search “Pet Allowed” homes, or contact us for options.

3. Do the apartments have a check-in desk or staff?

Some do, especially in serviced buildings. Some properties offer keyless access or a personal check-in with a host.

4. What should I do if I need to cancel or change my stay?

Many of our listings offer flexible cancellation. Look for “Flexible Cancellation” when browsing or contact support to confirm.

5. Can I get airport pickup arranged with the booking?

Some premium properties offer this as an add-on service. Ask our team at the time of booking, and we’ll coordinate it for you.

6. Do holiday rentals come with utilities and services?

Yes. Most holiday apartments listed on Nest in Galaxy include DEWA, internet, air conditioning, and regular cleaning services.

7. Can tourists legally rent holiday apartments in Dubai?

Absolutely. All our listings follow Dubai Tourism regulations for short-term stays and are licensed for tourist use.
