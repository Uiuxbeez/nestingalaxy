Frequently Asked Questions

1. What short stays can I book in Dubai?

You will find furnished flats, modern apartments, comfy studios, and roomy villas - great for any stay. You visit for a holiday, a business trip, or planning a longer stay, our properties in La Riviera Tower, Dubai Marina, and Ontario Tower, Business Bay - are designed to suit lifestyles accordingly.

2. Can I rent a property in Dubai just for a few days?

Absolutely! Book for a day, a week, or a month - whatever suits you.

3. Are these short-term rentals family-friendly?

Yes, many are. We provide baby chairs, extra beds, and locations close to parks and attractions. Properties like Azizi Mina, The Palm Jumeirah, ensure comfort for all ages.

4. Do your short-term rentals in Dubai include utilities and housekeeping?

Yes, all our short-term apartment rentals in Dubai cover utility bills. Housekeeping may be included for longer stays or available on request for shorter visits.

5. Where are your short-term rentals located?

Our rentals are located in some of Dubai’s most sought-after neighborhoods.

Popular areas include:
  • La Riviera Tower in Dubai Marina
  • Ontario Tower in Business Bay
  • Azizi Mina on The Palm Jumeirah

...and more! City vibes or beachside calm - we've got the perfect spot for you.

6. How to book a short-term rental in Dubai?

Select your preferred dates. See what is available, and book your stay instantly. Our platform is safe, simple to use.

4. Can I book an extended holiday stay?

Definitely. All our flats and apartments are fully furnished and ready, from a quick weekend escape to a long family holiday.
