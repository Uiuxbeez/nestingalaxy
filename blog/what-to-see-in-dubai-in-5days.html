What to See in Dubai in 5 Days

Francis
Feb 6, 2023

A short trip with luxury, culture, and special moments.

"I had just 36 hours in Dubai and zero plans—except one: to experience as much wonder as possible. No plans. No guide. Just curiosity, a backpack, and a last-minute booking. No regrets."

Hey traveler!

If you're planning your very first trip and wondering what to see in Dubai in 5 days, welcome to the club.

I had the same question before my recent Dubai trip, and now, I'm sharing my tried-and-tested itinerary with a few juicy tips, including where to stay (hint: Ontario Tower rocks!) and how to make your stay smooth with a short-term apartment rental in Dubai.

Day 1: Classic Dubai — Skyscrapers & Fountains

Start strong. You are in Downtown Dubai, - the WOW moment!

  • Burj Khalifa: First stop? Head straight to the top. The view is unreal. Try to book tickets for early morning or sunset.
  • Dubai Mall: Next door, you'll get lost in this mega-mall. I didn't just shop, I visited the Dubai Aquarium and grabbed a crazy milkshake at Black Tap.
  • Dubai Fountain Show: At night, the music + lights + water = pure magic.

Optional: Check if there's a performance at Dubai Opera nearby!

Day 2: Desert Safari Day

Looking for the real desert adventure?

  • Dune Bashing
  • Camel Rides
  • Sandboarding
  • Desert Camp BBQ with shows

Dress comfortably. Book with a reliable safari provider.

Day 3: Step Back in Time — Explore Old Dubai

Besides malls and skyscrapers, here' are your fun filled adventures.

  • Al Fahidi: Wind-tower buildings, charming cafés, and inspiring art corners
  • Abra Ride at Dubai Creek: An unmissable experience for only 1 AED.
  • Gold & Spice Souks: Bargain your heart out.

Add-on: Head to Miracle Garden, if its a winter.

Day 4: Adventure — Abu Dhabi — Easy Day Trip

A blend of culture, and art. Enjoy your short ride from Dubai.

  • Abu Dhabi Louvre Museum: A treat for art lovers or a cool escape from the heat.
  • Sheikh Zayed Mosque: Peaceful elegance with stunning architecture.
  • Ferrari (Optional): Thrilling rides and fun for adventure lovers.

Tip: Head out from Dubai early - either with a tour or by driving yourself.

Day 5: Chill by the Water — You've Earned It

Dubai's coastal charm with a day of sun, sea, and stunning views → Enjoy sun, sea, and breathtaking coastal views.

  • Dubai Marina: Wishing for a scenic brunch? Dubai Marina is the best for a brunch with a view.
  • JBR Beach: Sun, sea, water sports. What more?
  • The Palm: Dine at The Pointe, play at Atlantis, or enjoy city views from The View.

Optional Luxury: Try Aura Skypool, pricey but worth every dirham.

Where I Stayed: Ontario Tower, Business Bay

I chose a short-term apartment in Dubai. It made my trip feel so much more relaxed and fun.

Ontario Tower in Business Bay was my pick:

  • 10 mins to Burj Khalifa
  • Canal view + great cafes around
  • Roomy, tidy, and budget-friendly
  • Close to the metro, hassle-free travel

Other Areas You Can Consider

  • Palm Jumeirah: for beach vibes
  • Downtown Dubai: if you want luxury
  • Dubai Marina: for nightlife
  • JVC: for budget stays

Quick Travel Tips

How to plan a Dubai trip from India?

  • Book early (flights + visa)
  • Carry an AED or a forex card
  • Stay near the metro
  • Respect local customs

Is 5 days enough?

Yes. Definitely.

Final Word: Just Go!

Dubai is wild, wonderful, and worth it. Save this post, book your flights, grab a cozy spot at Nest in Galaxy's Ontario Tower, and trust me, you'll love every minute.
