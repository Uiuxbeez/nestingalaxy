Is Shopping in Dubai Expensive or Worth It?

Francis
Feb 6, 2023

Thinking about a shopping trip to Dubai? Dubai is every shopper's dream - but is it worth the splurge? The answer: it depends on what's in your shopping bag.

Luxury stores at The Dubai Mall or Mall of the Emirates have premium price tags. Not everything in Dubai will drain your wallet. Perfumes, gadgets, gold, and even a few fashion picks can be easier on the budget because of tax-free shopping and sales.

Visit during the Dubai Shopping Festival or Dubai Summer Surprises, and you might just grab your dream brand at 75% off. Splurge or bargain - it's all in the timing.

What's Famous in Dubai for Shopping?

Dubai is packed with must-buy items, but these top the list:

  • Gold & Jewellery — The Dubai Gold Souk is famous, not only for its purity, but also for variety and craftsmanship.
  • Perfumes & Oud — Authentic Arabian scents that won't break the bank compared to other countries.
  • Electronics — From smartphones to cameras, you'll often find better deals than abroad.
  • Luxury Brands — Big-name fashion labels can be more affordable here.

Street Shopping in Dubai

If mall prices aren't your style, head to Meena Bazaar, Karama Market, or Dragon Mart. From everyday wear to wedding outfits, you'll find plenty of variety, often at a fraction of mall costs. Street shopping in Dubai is especially loved by tourists hunting for affordable fashion and willing to bargain.

Where to Score Cheaper Deals

  • Fashion — Zara, H&M, Mango, and Aldo have great seasonal sale prices.
  • Electronics — Find special offers and festival deals at Jumbo and Sharaf DG.
  • Luxury — Rolex, Cartier, and Dior offer better prices during big shopping events in Dubai.

Are Clothes Expensive to Buy in Dubai?

A Zara tee might cost slightly more than in India, but a Gucci handbag could be hundreds less. While fast fashion isn't always cheaper than India, luxury shopping usually offers more value here.

Which Electronics are Cheap in Dubai?

Dubai is a hub for:

  • Smartphones — iPhone, Samsung, and OnePlus models
  • Laptops & Tablets — Wide range of brands and models
  • Cameras & Lenses
  • Smartwatches & Accessories

During GITEX Shopper, prices drop even further - and freebies are common.

Your Shopping Questions, Answered

  • Is Dubai Mall overpriced?
    It's luxury-focused. Yes, some stores in the mall are pricey. But it also has mid-range brands and seasonal sales.
  • Is $100 a day enough in Dubai?
    If you shop at souks and eat cheaply, $100 is enough. In malls, it won't last long.
  • Cheapest buys?
    Dates, spices, and small souvenirs.

Travel & Stay Tip

Is shopping your priority? Stay central for quick access to malls and markets.

The Bottom Line

Dubai isn't always cheap. But with smart shopping, it can be. Plan your visit for festivals and mix high-end malls with local souks for great deals.
