10 Don'ts in Dubai - Your Classy Traveler's Guide

Francis
Feb 6, 2023

So, you're jetting off to Dubai. Excellent choice!

Dubai's skyline sparkles, legendary brunches, and luxury are magnificent. May it be a Marina views from La Riviera Tower or relaxing by the pool at a villa in Dubai, one thing is clear - you're here for a world-class experience.

But even seasoned travelers can make mistakes.

Here's your easy guide on what not to do in Dubai as a tourist- so you can sip, shop, and shine without a single misstep.

1. Don't Confuse Desert Heat with Dress Code Freedom

Yes, the desserts are hot.

But no, think before you wander the mall in a bikini top or short-shorts. Dubai loves style, but modesty matters. Keep your dresses elegant and respectful.

Save the bold beach looks for private villas or beach clubs, where anything goes (well, almost).

2. Don't Go Full Romance in Public

You love the 'LOVE'- we get it. Public displays of love aren't welcomed in Dubai.

  • A quick hand-hold? Acceptable.
  • A romantic kiss at the fountain? Not so much.

Keep the cuddles to your suite.

3. Watch Your Language

Dubai is a city of grace, so mind your manners.

Even swearing or showing rude gestures - like in traffic - can get you into trouble. Stay calm and let your words - online and offline - reflect the city's elegance.

Let your words be polished & poised - just like the vibe.

4. Drink in Style, Not in Public

In Dubai, not all spaces are great for a drink.

There are many licensed spots, which can be your friendly spot for drinking. Never carry alcohol in public or appear drunk on the beach.

Keep your celebrations elegant and legal for a worry-free holiday.

5. Don't Snap First, Ask Later

Dubai is photogenic at every angle.

There are many unique places to visit in Dubai, like Al Fahidi District or the Dubai Frame, for your favourite shot. But always seek permission before photographing locals, especially women.

When in doubt, focus on the architecture - it's flawless anyway.

6. Respect Ramadan - Keep Meals Private in Daylight

Ramadan is celebrated across Dubai.

During the festival, avoid eating or drinking in public in a day. Enjoy your meals privately in luxury hotels or villas instead.

Let's respect the tradition.

7. Don't Assume Everything is Always Open

Dubai slows down at certain hours.

Prayer times and Fridays bring a different pace to Dubai. Check business hours before heading out to avoid surprises.

Plan well to save your time.

8. Don't Get Loud in Your Luxury Rental

Booking a stunning villa for rent in Dubai? Perfect.

But privacy doesn't mean party central. Keep the volume elegant, so neighbors and other families can enjoy their stay too.

After all, not everyone is noise-friendly.

9. Don't Travel with Just Any Medication

Certain medicines are restricted in Dubai.

Some drugs that are common at home may be prohibited in the UAE. Always review the rules and travel with your prescriptions.

Stay cautious. Stay safe.

10. Don't Skip the Culture

To truly experience Dubai's culture, one visit is never enough.

Dubai's culture is a perfect blend of Arab, Islamic, and Bedouin traditions touched by modern charm. Explore Al Fahidi's heritage lanes, browse vibrant souks, and step into peaceful mosques.

Truly feel the city's soul.

Final Word

Following Dubai's simple etiquette makes your journey smooth.

Sip coffee on a balcony, cruise the Palm, or relax in a luxury villa - just do it with grace. Embrace the cultural side, and your Dubai trip will become far more than just luxury.

Be ready for your next unforgettable adventure.
